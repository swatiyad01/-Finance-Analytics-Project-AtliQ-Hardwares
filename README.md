# AtliQ Hardwares – Finance & Sales Analytics Project

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the financial and sales performance of AtliQ Hardwares using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX.

The analysis was performed across multiple dimensions including Years, Months, Markets, Subzones, Products, and Customers to generate actionable business insights.

---

## Finance Analysis

### Key Insights

- Net Sales grew from **$196.7M to $598.9M** (**204% growth**)
- Gross Margin % declined from **41.4% to 36.4%**, indicating cost pressure
- Growth remained consistent across months with multiple high-growth spikes
- YoY comparison showed strong monthly growth (**150%–400%+**)
- India generated the highest revenue with moderate GM% (~32%)
- Profitability varied across subzones (~32%–42%+ GM%)

### Finance Analysis Performed

## Sales Analysis

### Key Insights

- Sales increased from **$196.7M (2020)** to **$598.9M (2021)**
- Products like **AQ Electron 4 3600** and **AQ Smash 2** achieved **500%+ YoY growth**
- Identified Top 5 and Bottom 5 products based on sales quantity and revenue
- India generated the highest revenue with **$161.3M sales in 2021**
- Customers like Amazon, AtliQ Exclusive, and Flipkart showed **200%–400%+ growth**
- New product launches contributed approximately **$176.2M revenue in 2021**
